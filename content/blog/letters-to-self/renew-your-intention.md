---
title: Renew Your Intention
date: 2024-07-10
---
How do you stay motivated all the time? How do you keep track of the **big picture** and not lose yourself into daily mundane activities that seem to drain too much energy sometimes? Why do some people stop chasing their aspirations and others don't?

The only way to keep on the path that you've actually chose is by renewing your intention.

By renewing it, you won't forget about it or let it dust for a long time.
By renewing it, you keep your commitment to it, and don't let the business of life to get in your way.
By renewing it, no matter how hard other things come at you or how low do you feel, you stick to it.

Renew your intention, daily.
